Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

1. Information We Process

AetherAI's free browser tools run directly on your device. Text entered into the AI Writer, Code Architect, Summarizer, Art Lab, or Voice Synth is processed locally in your browser and is not sold or shared with third-party data brokers.

We use browser localStorage solely to remember your selected visual theme (e.g., Cyber Violet, Emerald Matrix, or Solar Gold). You can clear this anytime via your browser settings.

3. Google AdSense & Third-Party Cookies

We may use Google AdSense to display advertisements on this site. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to AetherAI and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

4. Affiliate Links

Some outbound links in our "Recommended Pro AI Partners" section are affiliate links. Clicking those links may place a tracking cookie managed by the partner platform so AetherAI receives referral credit at no extra cost to you.

5. Payments (Razorpay)

Optional Pro plan upgrades are processed securely through Razorpay. AetherAI does not store your credit card, UPI PIN, or banking credentials on our servers.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to request data deletion, please reach out through our Contact page.
