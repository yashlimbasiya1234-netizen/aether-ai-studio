OUR MISSION

About AetherAI

Making fast, animated, privacy-friendly AI utilities accessible to creators, students, and developers worldwide.

Why We Built AetherAI

Modern AI software is often locked behind steep monthly paywalls, mandatory sign-up walls, and cluttered dashboards. AetherAI was designed to give creators an instant, distraction-free workspace where core tools work directly in the browser.

What We Offer

  • AI Content & Script Writer: Fast templates for Reels, blogs, emails, and ad copy.
  • Code & Regex Architect: Clean boilerplate snippets for web, Python, SQL, and regex validation.
  • Art & Prompt Lab: Studio-grade prompt enhancement paired with downloadable procedural canvas art.
  • Smart Summarizer: Instant reading metrics, keyword extraction, and bullet takeaways.
  • AI Voice Synth: Browser-native text-to-speech with a live animated equalizer.

How We Keep Core Tools Free

To keep AetherAI free for everyone, we partner with trusted AI platforms through clearly labeled affiliate recommendations, display non-intrusive advertisements, and offer optional Pro upgrades for teams and power users.
